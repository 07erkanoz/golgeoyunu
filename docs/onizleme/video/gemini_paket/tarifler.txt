=== VİDEO 1 · YEREBATAN'A DÜŞÜŞ ===
The Yerebatan cistern in Istanbul: rows of dark ancient columns rising from black still water, their reflections in the water, a large old stone Medusa head lying at the base of one column, half in the water. Saye falls from the darkness above, tumbling slowly, her red scarf fluttering, and splashes into the water with a soft splash and rings of ripples. Right after her, the small amber glass jar on its short string falls into the water nearby and floats; a few fireflies float above it. Saye sinks for a moment, then her head comes up above the water. She looks around, frightened, and starts to swim to the right toward a low stone ledge at the water's edge.

=== VİDEO 3 · UMAY'IN FİDANI VE KAVANOZ (ilk kare: 3_baslangic_karesi.png) ===
Animate the attached first frame. Saye stands on the stone ledge, dripping water. Right next to her, a tiny white birch sapling sprouts from a crack in the stone and grows into a small young birch tree with a few leaves; a single white cloth strip is tied on one of its branches, and the little tree glows softly with a pure white light (a sacred sign). Saye steps back, startled, then looks at it with wonder. The small amber glass jar floats on the water toward the ledge; Saye kneels and pulls it out by its string.
