VİDEO 1 · DÜŞÜŞ
Saye falls from the darkness above into the black water of the cistern, tumbling slowly, her red scarf fluttering. She splashes into the water with a soft splash and ripples. Right after her, a small amber glass jar on a short string falls into the water nearby and floats, a few fireflies spill out of it and float in the air. At the base of one column a large old stone Medusa head lies half in the water. Saye sinks for a moment, then her head comes up above the water; she looks around, frightened, and starts to swim to the right.

VİDEO 3 · UMAY'IN FİDANI VE KAVANOZ (başlangıç karesi: 2_son_kare.png)
Saye stands on the stone ledge, dripping. Right next to her, a tiny white birch sapling sprouts from a crack in the stone and grows into a small young birch tree with a few leaves; a single white cloth strip is tied on one of its branches and it glows softly with a pure white light (a sacred sign). Saye steps back, startled, then looks at it with wonder. The small amber glass jar floats on the water toward the ledge; Saye kneels and pulls it out by its string. The fireflies fly around her and one by one fly into the jar; the jar glows warm amber. She stands up, holding the glowing jar low in front of her by its string.

VİDEO 4 · KANDİLCİ VE YOLA ÇIKIŞ (başlangıç karesi: video 3'ün son karesi; ek: kandilci.png)
Far away in the background, between the columns, a tall hooded Lamplighter (the dark hooded figure in the attached image, with a staff and a cold mint-green glowing lantern) walks slowly from left to right; its cold light moves across the columns. Saye quickly crouches down and hides the glowing jar against her chest so its light is almost covered, and stays very still. The Lamplighter passes and disappears into the darkness. Saye slowly stands up, looks in the direction it went, holds the jar low in front of her and walks to the right with small careful steps, out of the frame to the right. The scene stays empty and dark for a moment.
